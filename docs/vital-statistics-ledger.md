# Vital Statistics Ledger (Draft)

A two-track approach to identity records: biological fact and legal
responsibility, kept separate so one never corrupts the other.

## Track A: Biological Origin (Strict Genetic Fact)

- Populated ONLY via verified sample matching ($A + B = C$).
- Remains "Unverified / Pending" if no sample is tested.

## Track B: Legal Responsibility & Guardianship (Social Contract)

- Records legal parents, adoptive parents, or guardians.
- Recognizes voluntary care, support, and custody without altering Track A.

## Active Citizen Record

- **Status A: Legally Active (Administrative Trust)** — Maintains access to
  basic civic services (IDs, voting, general benefits).
- **Status B: Biologically Unverified (Pending Physical Match)** — Flagged
  for sample collection during routine renewals or record updates.
